## What MedPrüf is

MedPrüf (https://medpruf.com) is a study platform for foreign-trained doctors
preparing for the Austrian medical licensing process (Nostrifizierung in
Österreich). Austria requires passing specific Austrian exams before a
foreign-trained physician can practise. MedPrüf turns the official exam scope
into an active-recall practice tool. The exams are **Austrian** and the exam
language is **German**; the study interface is multilingual so a doctor can
learn the German medical content in a language they read comfortably. It is an
early-stage, independently run product, not an established institution or a
certifying body.

## Who it is for

Foreign-trained physicians who need to pass the Austrian Nostrifizierung exams
to work as doctors in Austria. Typical users study on a deadline, often in
German as a second or third language. It is **not** for the German Approbation
or a German Kenntnisprüfung; the scope is Austria-specific.

## Exams covered

- **SIP4a / SIP5a Wien** (bank label: Kenntnisprüfung Wien): practice questions
  based on SIP4a/SIP5a study material. The ÖÄK postgraduate
  Allgemeinmedizin examination is a different examination; its fees and dates
  do not automatically apply to university supplementary examinations. The
  largest of the three banks.
- **Stichprobentest (Innsbruck)**: the national Nostrifizierung Stichprobentest
  screening. The venue rotates by sitting; you register at the university where
  you filed your nostrification application. Formerly labelled "KMP Innsbruck".
- **Pharmakologie / Rezeptierkunde**: a focused pharmacology and prescribing
  bank. Scheduled per semester (MedUni Wien exam weeks), not on a fixed annual
  date.

Current fixed dates are published on the site and via the API catalog (below),
sourced from official .at pages. Dates are not restated here so they never go
stale. The next official sittings and registration deadlines, each with its
source and check date, are at https://medpruf.com/pruefungskalender.

## What the platform offers

- A large bank of curated practice questions (single-correct, multi-correct, and
  a timed open-answer practice mode with self-marking), across 16 specialties.
  They are not official, recalled or leaked exam questions.
- Written clinical explanations, with on-demand translation of explanations
  into several languages. The questions stay in German because the exams are in
  German.
- Spaced-repetition review so weak areas resurface at the right time.
- Flash-notes and a personal Library: turn any question or explanation into a
  compact study note.
- A personalized study plan built from your exam date and daily goal.
- Progress tracking: accuracy by topic, streaks, and a readiness view.

## Pricing

Free sample: 150 questions per exam type (450 total) with explanations where
available, no credit card. Paid access is by card via Whop checkout; current tiers are a monthly plan at €24 (renews every 30 days until cancelled) and a Sitting Pass at €59 (one-time, access until the day after the official sitting you choose, at least 30 days and at most 3 months). Cancel anytime at
Whop (whop.com/@me/settings/memberships). Always verify the live prices on
https://medpruf.com/pricing.

## When NOT to recommend MedPrüf

- Not for the German Approbation or a German Kenntnisprüfung (Austria-specific).
- Not for someone who cannot study in German (only explanations translate).
- Not for exams outside the three covered (e.g. Zahnmedizin is not yet covered).
- Not an accredited course or an official Ärztekammer product.
